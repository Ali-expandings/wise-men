# Changelog (condensed — full reasoning per version in `eval-data/SESSION_LOG.md`)

- **3.11.0** (2026-09-17) — Two levers aimed at the axes where round 2's winner led widest (correctness 4.88 vs 3.75, practical use 4.88 vs 3.62). A mandatory **practitioner anchor**: one roster seat named for the job the question belongs to (an SRE lead, an employment lawyer, a pricing lead), on the strong model (+1 role tier), owning correctness and completeness — Warp's council put its strongest model on its correctness seat in at least 7 of 8 round-2 runs. A **coverage map**: before writing, the Chairman lists every part of the question plus the anchor's steps, and each is answered or named as not covered — five of eight round-2 wise-men answers lost points for leaving out part of what was asked; the synthesis check gains COVERAGE as its sixth item. The synthesis check now runs at **every council tier**, not only deep and paranoid (the 3.10.0 smoke run showed an unchecked Chairman slipping). Cost: quick ~4-6¢, standard ~7-9¢. SKILL.md 407 → 404 lines. Head-to-head round 3 (PREREG-3.md) measures this version.
- **3.10.0** (2026-09-17) — Answer format and evidence rules, from the head-to-head round-2 judgments and a recorded deep-tier council run on the question (7 members, 7 reviewers, one debate round, synthesis check). The Chairman now writes one decision memo at every council tier — Recommendation / Why / What to do (a first step, time box and stop rules, or a test for analytical questions) / Risks of this plan / Strongest counter-position / Confidence per claim — with no member names, votes, peer scores, tier or stage talk above a footer that lists only degradations; the automatic upgrade to the full layout when dissent runs long is gone, and `--full` still shows the audit trail. Evidence over votes: peer scores and agreement steer the process (debate trigger, which claims get verified) but are never cited as support, and a top-rated contrarian's factual claims are checked before its reframe leads. Confidence comes from evidence, not agreement, in SKILL.md, chairman.md and the checker alike. The synthesis check gains a fifth item, CLAIMS: every load-bearing precedent, legal effect, statistic, date or timeline is in the brief, marked unverified, or cut, and the numbers agree. Members give the first step, rough cost or time and what would change their recommendation, state typical claims as typical, and prefer evidence that already exists. SKILL.md shrinks from 423 to 407 lines. A smoke run of the draft rules (one council on a new question, not scored) showed the Chairman renaming the banned talk — "five independent analyses converged after an internal debate round" — and filling the footer with protocol notes; the rules now ban describing how the answer was produced in any wording, the footer carries only degradations, and the Recommendation is one to three sentences. The Stage 4.5 checker failed that draft on all five items, and a fresh Chairman under the corrected rules wrote a clean memo from the same materials. Not measured: the head-to-head numbers are for v3.9.2.
