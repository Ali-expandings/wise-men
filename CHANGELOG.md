Default 23.8/25 and `--fast` 23.3 against llm-council 17.9, Warp's council 17.5 and a plain answer 16.3 — both configurations clearly ahead of every rival on all five questions (+5.4 to +7.5, every interval excludes zero). Not won: Warp is the quickest and cheapest council (9.3 min, $2.03, against the fast profile's 10.8 and $2.09); the default is the slowest and dearest arm (37.3 min, $9.59; the synthesis check takes 9–11 minutes of every default run, deep-tier reviewers 5–9). The rule said to state what changed in the council record; with no record to write, orchestrators put it in the answer. Now: what the check found and changed is logged in the record or nowhere, a footer exists only for real degradations and facts corrected against a source, the checker fails any line about the check, tier or mode, and "the council's…" joins the banned phrasings. When no tier is named, the first status line now says which tier is running, its usual time, and that `--fast` exists. Results: `eval-data/head-to-head/RESULTS-V4.md`. 3.13.1 itself is unmeasured.

- **3.10.0** (2026-09-17) — Answer format and evidence rules, from the head-to-head round-2 judgments and a recorded deep-tier council run on the question (7 members, 7 reviewers, one debate round, synthesis check). The Chairman now writes one decision memo at every council tier — Recommendation / Why / What to do (a first step, time box and stop rules, or a test for analytical questions) / Risks of this plan / Strongest counter-position / Confidence per claim — with no member names, votes, peer scores, tier or stage talk above a footer that lists only degradations; the automatic upgrade to the full layout when dissent runs long is gone, and `--full` still shows the audit trail. Evidence over votes: peer scores and agreement steer the process (debate trigger, which claims get verified) but are never cited as support, and a top-rated contrarian's factual claims are checked before its reframe leads. Confidence comes from evidence, not agreement, in SKILL.md, chairman.md and the checker alike. The synthesis check gains a fifth item, CLAIMS: every load-bearing precedent, legal effect, statistic, date or timeline is in the brief, marked unverified, or cut, and the numbers agree. Members give the first step, rough cost or time and what would change their recommendation, state typical claims as typical, and prefer evidence that already exists. SKILL.md shrinks from 423 to 407 lines. A smoke run of the draft rules (one council on a new question, not scored) showed the Chairman renaming the banned talk — "five independent analyses converged after an internal debate round" — and filling the footer with protocol notes; the rules now ban describing how the answer was produced in any wording, the footer carries only degradations, and the Recommendation is one to three sentences. The Stage 4.5 checker failed that draft on all five items, and a fresh Chairman under the corrected rules wrote a clean memo from the same materials.
