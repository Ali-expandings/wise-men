## MEMBER: Empiricist (sonnet-4.6)
Core: Weak-to-moderate internal validity, low external validity. 12pt modest; N=80 underpowered, true effect plausibly 4-20; single-site can't separate diet from site confounders. Don't generalize beyond "promising signal worth replicating." Risks: underpowering (male subgroup ~25-30 = noise), single-city confounding, 8-week regression-to-baseline, publication bias (12pt = exactly clears pub bar), demographic restriction (40-55 women hormonal/metabolic specific). Rec: hypothesis-confirming not practice-changing; next = multi-site RCT N≥300 balanced-sex 6mo+ pre-registered. Conf low-moderate effect real in-population, low it replicates at 12pt, very low cross-demographic. Weakest: 8-week reduction = durable mechanistic vs novelty/Hawthorne + compliance that degrades post-trial.

## MEMBER: Theorist (sonnet-4.6)
Core: Internal validity plausible for sample; external genuinely constrained. Mechanism (dietary change → lipid metabolism) well-established biologically so DIRECTION trustworthy; MAGNITUDE doesn't travel freely. Risks: sex-hormone interaction (estrogen upregulates hepatic LDL receptors — diet interacts with hormonal backdrop differing by sex/age), single-city confound (effect size = function of how far sample moved from baseline), short horizon (8wk = acute response only), small-N wide CI (±5-8pt), healthy-volunteer recruitment bias. Rec: finding = evidence MECHANISM works not portable number; justify larger multi-site; don't plug 12pt into cost-effectiveness as fixed; if must use, discount 30-50% for adherence decay + heterogeneity. Conf direction ~85%, magnitude ~25-35%. Weakest: sample's baseline dietary pattern (the delta produced) representative of target — effect size in diet RCT almost entirely function of how bad starting diet was.

## MEMBER: Methodologist (sonnet-4.6)
Core: Internal validity reasonable for RCT this size; external substantially constrained. 12pt real signal within studied population; design doesn't support population-level claims. Generalize cautiously + narrowly. Risks: gender skew (female physiology dominates), age banding (40-55 specific window), geographic/cultural confound (single US city — baseline diet/water/stress/access/ethnicity unmeasured), N=80 precision (CI 5-18, winner's curse overestimate), 8-week no durability, adherence artifacts (high researcher contact vs real-world). Rec: limit to women 40-55 no-comorbidity similar-dietary-context 8-week; need replication multi-site + longer follow-up + pre-registered subgroup. Conf moderate-high something real in sample, low-moderate 12pt replicates general. Weakest: sample representative of any population beyond itself — single-site narrow RCT assumes local context irrelevant, almost certainly false for diet (baseline diet/food environment/cultural norms = direct moderators).

## MEMBER: Integrator (sonnet-4.6)
Core: 12pt real signal worth taking seriously, generalization radius narrow. "Promising hypothesis" not "established fact." Safe to generalize: dietary mechanism probably operates in broadly similar metabolic populations. Unsafe: magnitude, men, older/younger, non-Western diets, different baseline cholesterol. Risks: sex/hormone confound (estrogen affects metabolism — may not hold in men), single-site homogeneity (intervention may be partially local context), N=80 underpowers subgroups, 8wk not durability, publication bias (12pt = high end of distribution). Rec: generalize DIRECTION (diet class likely lowers LDL middle-aged adults), not MAGNITUDE (12pt); before acting look for male-majority replication + longer follow-up + meta-analysis; treat true effect as "0-12, probably 5-9." Conf medium-low direction broadly, low magnitude outside demographic. Weakest: "cholesterol physiology universal" — modulated by sex hormones, ApoE genetic variants, gut microbiome, baseline diet, all varying systematically across populations.

## MEMBER: Devil's Advocate (sonnet-4.6)
Core: Standard "small/narrow sample, can't generalize" reflex is itself a bias destroying useful signal. N=80 8-week RCT is not a pilot — it's a completed RCT. Cholesterol metabolism not culturally constructed; biochemical pathways (dietary fat/fiber/sterols → LDL/HDL) conserved across sexes/ethnicities. 12pt ≈ low-dose statin. MECHANISM not demographic drives generalizability. Reflexive dismissal = epistemic cowardice dressed as rigor. Risk (over-conservatism failure mode): wait for perfect multi-site age-balanced 2000-person trial (7 years, $40M) while real replicated effect that could reduce CV events in millions sits on shelf; clinicians dismissing every adequately-powered RCT on demographic grounds default to pharma with thinner mechanistic justification. Rec: generalize direction + plausibility broadly + immediately; apply demographic caveats only to effect SIZE; recommend as trial-worthy option outside studied demographic while flagging magnitude varies — rather than withholding pending fuller evidence. Conf high mechanism generalizes, moderate exact 12pt, low that "wait for better data" beats cautious application now. Weakest: biochemical pathways homogeneous enough across unstudied groups — if works partly through gut microbiome (varies by geography/prior diet), one-city homogeneity is genuine confounder not just demographic checkbox.
