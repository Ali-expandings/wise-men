## MEMBER: Empiricist (sonnet-4.6)
Core: Hypothesis generator not conclusion. Gray-lit fraction shifts burden of proof; key empirical Q = whether gray studies have systematically different effect sizes than published. Risks: publication-bias amplification not correction (file drawer of never-written nulls still invisible), heterogeneous quality as variance, selective retrieval (effort/language/network), small-study effects drive funnel asymmetry, trim-and-fill/Egger underpowered <20 studies + high heterogeneity. Rec: run published-only vs full sample; if effect survives published-only, confidence rises; check effect-size vs sample-size correlation. Conf moderate-high on failure modes. Weakest: that gray studies systematically lower-quality — direction depends on WHY unpublished (could be withheld industry trials or govt nulls).

## MEMBER: Theorist (sonnet-4.6)
Core: Weak prior not evidence. MA assumes study pool ≈ random sample of all studies; gray lit introduces DIFFERENT non-random filter (what reaches repositories/abstracts/theses), not orthogonal to effect. Narrow CI = false confidence (reflects pooled N not epistemic reliability). Risks: file-drawer (Rosenthal 1979), quality-filter collapse (no peer-review friction), heterogeneous operationalization (apples/oranges amplified), availability bias in gray search, trim-and-fill false reassurance, inverse-variance weighting still shifts upward. Rec: stratify published vs gray; fail-safe N vs domain plausibility; I²>75% → uninterpretable; moderator analysis by quality; Bayesian prior discount (published-only = posterior mean, headline = upper bound). Conf high on risk ID. Weakest: gray=positive-biased; in regulatory/tox fields gray lit may CORRECT bias (mandatory null reporting). Check domain.

## MEMBER: Methodologist (sonnet-4.6)
Core: Moderate-to-high skepticism, don't dismiss. Effect probably real if survives sensitivity, but magnitude inflated + CI too narrow. Risks: publication-bias amplification (gray search catches preliminary-positives (b) more than file-drawer-nulls (a) — biased convenience sample leaning positive), heterogeneous quality masking, gray search not reproducible (network/access/language — methods section buries this), selective inclusion from gray sources (favors studies with extractable = big effects), funnel asymmetry uninterpretable once gray added, fail-safe N meaningless. Rec: demand (1) trim-and-fill WITHOUT gray — does effect survive?; (2) subgroup by quality/registration; (3) exact operationalization of gray search (databases/dates/languages/thresholds). If absent → effect = upper bound. Conf high on direction, moderate on application. Weakest: gray searches REDUCE bias rather than replace one filter with another — only prospective registration captures true file drawer.

## MEMBER: Integrator (sonnet-4.6)
Core: Hypothesis-generating signal not confirmatory. Gray inclusion = virtue in intent (combat pub bias), landmine in execution. Critical variable = whether gray retrieved systematically + quality assessed independently of results. MA where effect DISAPPEARS after gray inclusion = more trustworthy than one where it appears only because of it. Risks: selective gray retrieval (convenience = curated subsample, inflate OR deflate arbitrarily), asymmetric quality floors (unpublished has no floor; large-N flawed dissertation dominates if weighted by N), heterogeneity masking (I² jumps; pooled effect = artifact of averaging incompatible populations), synthesis-level p-hacking (inclusion/coding/model degrees of freedom), funnel uninterpretable. Rec: (1) subgroup split — effect hold in published alone?; (2) trim-and-fill/p-curve on published subset; (3) scrutinize retrieval protocol (named databases/strings/author-contact = credible; vague = steep discount). Conf moderately high on framework. Weakest: "unpublished" = "suppressed null" — much gray is unpublished for non-result reasons (budget/pilot/govt norms); MA should code reason-for-non-publication, most don't.

## MEMBER: Devil's Advocate (sonnet-4.6)
Core: Take it MORE seriously than instinct says. Reflexive "gray lit = junk" is itself documented bias. Including unpublished is methodologically CORRECT — combats publication bias, the single largest distorting force. MA that EXCLUDED gray is the one to distrust. Gray presence isn't red flag — it's evidence authors did their job. Risks: asymmetric quality filtering (published passed peer review, gray didn't; small-N bespoke-measure study weighted with N=800 validated-instrument RCT → noise injection dressed as correction), funnel plot manipulation (selecting gray to fill left side creates symmetric funnel passing Egger while underlying asymmetry remains). Rec: demand forest plot by publication status; run MA twice (published-only vs full); similar magnitude both = genuine corroboration; effect only emerges with gray = load-bearing scrutiny needed. Conf high framework, moderate on this MA's failure mode. Weakest: peer review as reliable quality floor — in nutrition/psych/some medical fields published lit so contaminated by p-hacking/HARKing that published=better premise collapses.
