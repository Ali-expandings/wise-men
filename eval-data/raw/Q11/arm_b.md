## Answer

30 paying B2B customers with no marketing is a real PMF signal, but it's not sufficient to justify a full Head of Marketing hire yet. Here is the integrated reasoning:

**What the 30 customers actually tell you:** They prove that the product can get paid for. They do not prove you have a repeatable acquisition motion, a clear ICP, or messaging that scales. Before a marketing leader can do anything useful, they need those inputs — and right now, you have them locked inside customer conversations you probably haven't had systematically yet.

**The right pre-hire move:** Do 20-30 structured customer interviews first (2-4 weeks). Extract: how they heard about you, what job they were hiring the product for, the exact language they use to describe the problem and solution. This produces positioning, ICP clarity, and the "marketing brief" a good Head of Marketing will demand on day one anyway. Skipping this step means hiring someone expensive to do discovery you should own as a founder.

**Then: should you hire a Head or not?** At 30 customers, the typical B2B SaaS arc is still sales-led. The higher-leverage hire is usually a demand gen IC, a growth-focused SDR, or a fractional CMO — not a full Head of Marketing at $150-200k+ who needs budget, headcount, and a clear mandate to be effective. A Head of Marketing hired without a proven channel will spend 6 months experimenting and then leave frustrated, or build the wrong thing.

**The exception that could flip this:** If your 30 customers came from one tight ICP, churn is low, NPS is high, and ACV is meaningful (say, $15k+), then you do have enough signal to bring in a senior marketing leader and point them at category creation and pipeline. In that case, hire sooner — good marketing leaders take 4-6 months to close, and category framing compounds early. But be honest about whether that describes you.

**Concrete recommendation:** Run customer interviews now. In parallel, audit where your 30 customers actually came from. If a clear pattern emerges (same industry, same pain, same acquisition source), start the Head of Marketing search. If the 30 are scattered across verticals and entry points, hire a demand gen IC first and treat the HoM search as a Q3/Q4 move.

## Dissent worth keeping

The obvious answer is "wait for more PMF signals," but that is procrastination dressed as rigor. 30 paying customers who found you with zero marketing IS the signal — customers are paying without being asked twice. The gap between 30 and 300 is a marketing gap, not a product gap. Every month you don't hire marketing, you are leaving compounding pipeline on the table while competitors are not. Good marketing leaders take 4-6 months to hire and onboard — starting the search now and closing in parallel with your customer discovery means you lose nothing and gain everything. Waiting for "more signals" is a habit that keeps B2B SaaS companies small.
