## Answer

Don't hire a Head of Marketing yet — but the reason isn't "wait for more PMF signal." 30 paying B2B customers with zero marketing is a real signal: those customers found you, evaluated you, and paid without being pushed. That's organic pull, cleaner than marketing-assisted growth because there's no spend noise in it.

The load-bearing question is **ACV**, and you have to answer it before anything else. A Head of Marketing costs $150-250K fully loaded. If your ACV is $5K, you're at $150K ARR and that single hire consumes your entire revenue base — mathematically suicidal. If your ACV is $50K, you're at $1.5M ARR and the conversation is genuinely different. Run that number first.

Assuming a typical early-stage ACV, the sequencing that works (and that Salesforce, HubSpot, and Veeva all followed) is: founder-led sales to ~50-100 customers → codify the repeatable motion → then add marketing to amplify it. Marketing amplifies a known motion; it does not discover one. Hiring a senior marketing leader before you have a proven channel means they spend six months building foundations (brand, content calendar, HubSpot instance) and then either leave frustrated or — worse — manufacture pipeline for the wrong segment.

Concrete plan:
1. **Interview your 30 customers (2-4 weeks).** How did they find you, what job were they hiring the product for, what was the buying trigger, why you over doing nothing. Do this yourself — it's the marketing brief any good hire will demand on day one.
2. **Close the next 10 yourself and document every deal** — channel, trigger, objection, close. This is the repeatable-motion data.
3. **Check retention.** If month-6 churn is already a problem, marketing spend accelerates the bleed, not growth. Fix retention first.
4. **Hire a scrappy demand-gen / growth operator ($80-120K), not a VP/Head.** Someone who runs channel experiments, not someone who builds a department. Title inflation here is how you burn $200K and get a brand refresh.
5. **Gate the actual Head of Marketing hire** at ~$1-2M ARR or 100+ customers with a proven, under-resourced channel — whichever comes first.

The exception that flips this: if your 30 customers came from one tight ICP, churn is low, NPS is high, and ACV is $15K+, you have enough signal to start a senior search now (good leaders take 4-6 months to close, and category framing compounds early). Be honest about whether that actually describes you.

## Dissent worth keeping

Devil's Advocate, preserved: the real danger is not hiring too early in the abstract — it's that a Head of Marketing hired at this stage MANUFACTURES PMF signals through channel optimization rather than surfacing genuine ones. Concrete failure: a $180K HoM instruments HubSpot, reports "MQL pipeline up 4x" in six months, while month-6 cohort churn quietly sits at 22% because the product has a workflow gap for the exact segment they targeted — a segment the founder would have caught by doing the first ten sales calls personally. Pipeline gets pre-filled with the wrong buyer, the sales cycle lengthens, CAC balloons, and unwinding the ICP assumption costs twelve months. The hire didn't fail; it succeeded at the wrong thing. And once a HoM owns the metrics, they own the internal interpretation of what "PMF" even means — founders cede that authority earlier than they realize.
