## MEMBER: User Advocate

Risks: senior marketer needs infra/budget/clarity you can't give, churns 12mo; acceleration before product retains at scale doubles churn destroys social proof; "founder relationship" customers bought because of YOU not product, marketer can't replicate. ## MEMBER: Devil's Advocate
Hiring HoM now WRONG — but obvious "get more PMF first" too soft. Real danger NOT hiring too early: HoM hired at this stage MANUFACTURES PMF signals through channel optimization rather than surfacing genuine ones. Concrete: $180K HoM instruments HubSpot, "MQL pipeline up 4x" in 6mo, meanwhile month-6 cohort churn 22% bc product workflow gap for the segment they targeted — segment founder would've caught doing first 10 calls. Pipeline pre-filled with wrong buyer, sales cycle lengthens, CAC balloons, unwinding ICP costs 12mo. Hire succeeded at wrong thing. Secondary: HoM owns interpretive authority over what "PMF" means internally. Rec: ask "do we understand WHY these 30 bought+stayed" not "enough PMF"; 15 customer dev interviews; hire marketing MANAGER not VP/Head. Conf 75%. Weakest: 30 customers meaningfully heterogeneous (if homogeneous, clean ICP signal exists).

## MEMBER: Historian
"Default alive through pull." Pattern at $0-1M ARR pre-marketing favors head of SALES or sales-led motion before head of marketing. Salesforce, HubSpot, Veeva all: founder-led sales to ~50-100 customers → VP Sales → marketing infra once repeatable playbook to amplify. Marketing too early = Gurley's "premature scaling" — damage is org confusion not wasted spend. Risks: hiring before codifying what works, HoM(brand) vs demand-gen(growth) archetype mismatch, opportunity cost (CS/sales hire compounds faster). Rec: 2-wk sprint interview 8-10 customers, apply Sean Ellis 40%-very-disappointed test, hire demand-gen operator with $1-10M ARR-stage experience not VP/brand. Conf medium-high. Weakest: 30 came through opaque/unscalable channels (if single identifiable channel, codify-first weakens).

Weakest: 30 came organic vs founder personal network/single enterprise relationship.
