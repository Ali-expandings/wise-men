30 paying customers in B2B SaaS without marketing is actually a strong signal — that's organic pull, not a leaky bucket you're trying to fill with spend. The customers found you; that matters.

But "head of marketing" is probably the wrong frame at your stage. Here's the honest breakdown:

At 30 customers you're still in discovery mode. A great head of marketing will ask you: "What's the ICP? What's the repeatable motion? What made those 30 convert?" If you can't answer those crisply, you'll waste their time and your money. Senior marketing hires at this stage often churn out in 12-18 months because the foundation isn't there yet — they show up wanting to run campaigns and build brand, but the company actually needs someone sitting in on sales calls figuring out messaging.

What you probably need first is a few more things to be true: you should be able to describe your best 5-10 customers in a sentence that would make a stranger say "oh, I know exactly who that is." You should have a rough sense of where those 30 customers came from and why they picked you over doing nothing. You should have some signal on retention — are these customers sticking? Expanding? If churn is already a problem, marketing spend accelerates bleeding, not growth.

If those things are mostly true, the next hire isn't necessarily a head of marketing — it's often a growth-oriented founder (you, leaning in more) plus maybe a PMM or demand gen contractor who can run 2-3 acquisition experiments cheaply over 90 days. That gets you the repeatable motion data you need to actually give a head of marketing something to scale.

Hire a head of marketing when you have a channel that's working but under-resourced, not when you're still figuring out what works. Right now it sounds like you're still in the latter. The fact that you have 30 paying customers without marketing is your leverage — use the next 60-90 days to interview those customers obsessively, understand the common thread, and run one or two cheap acquisition experiments before adding a senior salary to your burn.
