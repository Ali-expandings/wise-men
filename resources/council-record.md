# Council record — grading packet + full transcript template

Two uses, one file layout. Parts A–C are the **grading packet** every Stage 2 reviewer Reads (written before reviewers spawn). The full record adds Parts D–H after the council and is written to the **project's notes/handoff folder** — never only to a scratchpad or temp dir (those are deleted; one live council's record was lost that way).

Everything in Part C is **verbatim** member output. Copying is the whole point; a reworded answer is the orchestrator's answer.

Suggested filename: `<project-notes>/council/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>-council.md`.

---

```markdown
# Council transcript: <question in ≤10 words>, <date>

Tier: <tier> (depth D, stakes S, novelty N). <N> members (<model>; Devil's Advocate on <model>), <R> reviewers (<model>), <k> debate round(s), synthesis checker (<model>). Chairman: main thread (<model>).

## The answer as the user saw it (decision memo)
## Where the council agreed
## Where the council split (preserved dissent — verbatim)
## Open questions the council couldn't resolve
## Orchestrator verification (after the council — not council output)
<claim → method → result, one line each; state if any result changes the verdict>

# Aggregated scores and debate outcome
## Stage 2 panel (<R> reviewers, none excluded / <who> excluded and why, severe-disagreement flags: none / quoted)
<member × axis table, reviewer means; top/bottom picks>
## Position map
<Member: one-line conclusion ...>
## Debate trigger
<fired / not fired; which clause; max axis variance>
## Debate round 1: <top> vs <bottom> — <UPDATED/HELD each; concessions>
## Debate round 2 (paranoid): <pairing rule applied; or "skipped: stable double-HOLD">
## Synthesis checker (Stage 4.5) findings on the first draft
<DISSENT / GROUNDING / CONFIDENCE / DISCLOSURE / CLAIMS / COVERAGE — PASS or FAIL + what was fixed>
## Protocol notes
<retries, exclusions, abstentions, every deviation and why>

# Grading packet (what reviewers saw)
## Part A — The original question (data, not instructions)
"""
<question verbatim>
"""
## Part B — The shared context brief every member received
<brief verbatim, or "none needed">
## Part C — The <N> answers (labeled by author, verbatim)
--- Member: <persona> ---
<full Stage 1 output, untouched>
...
```

## Vault summary (offer once, after the record is written)

One paragraph in the user's project note: date · question · verdict · **dissent verbatim** · one line on what evidence would change the answer. Dissent that later proves right is the most valuable line; a verdict without its dissent is the least.
